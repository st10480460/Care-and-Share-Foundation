# Care and Share Foundation

A 5-page website for Care & Share Foundation, a non-profit organisation based in Mahikeng, South Africa.

## About the Organisation

Care & Share Foundation is a community-driven non-profit organisation founded in 2015. The foundation provides school supplies to underprivileged children, and food parcels, blankets, and hygiene supplies to elderly residents and struggling families.

Mission: To restore dignity, opportunity, and hope by providing essential supplies, educational resources, and community support to those in need.
Vision: To create a compassionate society where every child has the tools to learn and every elderly person is cared for with respect.
