Our Work

Explore the programs and initiatives we run to support our community.

School Supplies Drive

We provide essential learning materials to underprivileged children so they can attend school with confidence and dignity.

  • School shoes and uniforms
  • Stationery and textbooks
  • Backpacks and school bags

Food Parcel Distribution

We regularly distribute food parcels, blankets, and hygiene supplies to elderly residents and struggling families in Mahikeng.

  • Non-perishable food items
  • Warm blankets and clothing
  • Hygiene and sanitation products

Community Workshops

We offer educational programs and skills development workshops to empower underprivileged families.

  • Financial literacy training
  • Entrepreneurship workshops
  • Health and wellness education

Elderly Care Program

We provide ongoing support and companionship to elderly individuals who are isolated or in need.

  • Regular home visits
  • Meal delivery services
  • Healthcare referrals and support
