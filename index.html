Who We Are

Founded in 2015, Care & Share Foundation is a community-driven non-profit organization dedicated to helping underprivileged individuals and elderly people in Mahikeng and surrounding areas.

We provide essential resources such as school shoes, uniforms, stationery, food parcels, blankets, and hygiene supplies to those who need it most.
